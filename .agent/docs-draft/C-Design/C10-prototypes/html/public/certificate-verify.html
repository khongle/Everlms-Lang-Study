Common questions about certificates

How do I get my certificate code?

After completing a course, your certificate is automatically issued. The code appears on the certificate PDF and in your account under My Learning → Certificates.

Do certificates expire?

EverLMS certificates do not expire. Once issued, they remain verifiable on the blockchain indefinitely.

Can employers verify certificates for free?

Yes. The certificate verification portal is completely free and requires no account. Simply enter the certificate code.
