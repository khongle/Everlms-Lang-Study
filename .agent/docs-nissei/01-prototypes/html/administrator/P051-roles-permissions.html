Roles & permissions

Seven roles, six route groups, and the scope each role holds: the dimension that does not exist in a course-selling LMS.

Why scope is a third dimension. The base LMS model has two levels of ownership: your own data, or all data. This product adds branch in between: a role can be handed everything at one factory without being handed every factory. That single addition is why site_training_manager and hr_hse_central exist as two separate roles instead of one “manager” role. See the note below the matrix.

Why two roles, not oneSite Training Manager and HR/HSE Central hold identical Workspace permissions (assign training, manage employees, issue certificates, chase overdue lists) at different scope: one branch versus all five. In the original EverLMS role model both would have been a single “manager” role. Nissei splits them because the branch dimension makes that a meaningfully different grant of access, not a UI preference.

What administrator does not inherit

Administrator reaches every route group, but day-to-day business work still belongs to the business roles. Editing the training matrix, approving content or assigning courses is done as HR/HSE Central, not as Administrator. This account is scoped to configuration and security, and used sparingly, per the EverLMS least-privilege guidance this product inherits.

Where scope is edited

This page is a read view of the role model. Branch-level technical scope (timezone, locale, data region) is configured at Branch configuration. Individual account role assignment is not modelled as a separate prototype screen; it happens through the same employee record used across the system (see Employees).
