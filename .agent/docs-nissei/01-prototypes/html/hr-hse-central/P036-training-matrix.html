What this matrix drives

  • Every employee’s required course list and due dates
  • The due and overdue worklists at each site
  • Reminder emails and in-app notifications
  • Compliance rate on every dashboard and report

Nothing in this product is assigned by hand as a rule. Editing one cell here changes obligations for everyone holding that job role at that branch.
