What the programme covers

Statutory occupational safety training under Decree 44/2016 for the Vietnamese factories, plus quality, environmental and conduct training across the group. Course content itself is available after sign-in.

SAF Occupational safety

General safety, working at height, electrical safety, machine operation, forklift, chemical handling, first aid.

Groups 3 and 4

FIR Fire safety

Fire prevention and evacuation, refreshed every 12 months at every site.

QMS Quality and environment

ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 awareness, aligned with the certifications the sites already hold.

CMP Conduct and security

Code of conduct, and cybersecurity awareness for office and supervisory staff.

Scope rule made visibleThis is the entire public surface: a landing page, certificate verification, and contact details. There is no public course catalog, no marketing site and no self-registration, because this is an internal system. It exists as a page at all because two audiences legitimately arrive without an account: inspectors verifying a certificate, and employees on their way to sign in. Whether even the course titles should be visible before sign-in is an open question for the customer (the comparable deployment at another Japanese group leaves its catalog public; a stricter posture may not).
