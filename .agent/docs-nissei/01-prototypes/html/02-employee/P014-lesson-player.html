Spill response

A chemical spill on the production floor follows the same sequence every time, regardless of which chemical is involved: alert and evacuate, contain before you clean, protect yourself before you approach, use the correct absorbent, then report. The Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for the chemical tells you which of these steps changes for that specific substance.

  1. Alert and evacuate. Call out to nearby workers, stop any process feeding the spill, and clear non-essential personnel from the immediate area.
  2. Contain before you clean. Use absorbent booms or berms from the nearest spill station to stop the spill spreading toward drains or aisles.
  3. Protect yourself first. Put on the PPE listed on the SDS for that chemical class before you approach, even for a small spill.
  4. Use the correct absorbent. Spill kits at NEV-HCM are colour coded by chemical class; the wrong absorbent can react with some solvents instead of neutralising them.
  5. Log and report. Every spill, however small, is reported to HSE before the end of the shift so it is captured in the site’s chemical incident record.
  • Never clean an unknown chemical without checking the SDS first. Guessing the wrong response can turn a small spill into an exposure incident.
  • Spill kits are staged at fixed points on the floor plan. Know the nearest one to your station before you need it, not after.
