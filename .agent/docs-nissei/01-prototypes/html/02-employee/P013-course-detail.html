Why this course is required

Every employee in the job role “Production Operator” at branch NEV-HCM is required to hold a current SAF-105 First Aid & Emergency Response certificate. This rule comes from the training matrix, not from a manager’s choice: it is one row of the position × course grid maintained centrally by HR/HSE (job role “Production Operator” × course “SAF-105”), set to a 12-month retraining cycle. First aid certification has no Decree 44/2016 group classification; it applies on top of the statutory safety groups because production floor injuries need an immediate first responder on every shift.
