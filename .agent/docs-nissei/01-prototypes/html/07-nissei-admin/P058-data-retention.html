Cross-border and local data rules

Vietnam · NEV-HCM, NEH-HAN, NEM-MTO

5,800 employees

Personal data collected from Vietnamese staff for training compliance is processed under Decree 13/2023 on personal data protection: purpose limitation to training and safety compliance, data subject notice, and the retention periods above. Data for these three branches stays in the ap-southeast (Vietnam) data region set at Branch configuration.

China · NES-SHA, NES-SZN

42 employees

Training records for the Shanghai and Shenzhen trading offices are treated as HR-management personal information, which PIPL exempts from a separate cross-border consent for each individual transfer when the purpose is legally required human resource management such as occupational training records. Onward transfer and security assessment obligations still apply if data volume or sensitivity thresholds are crossed.

Right to erasure versus the duty to keep evidence. An employee’s request to erase their personal data can conflict directly with the statutory duty to retain occupational safety training evidence for the periods above. This system does not auto-delete training or certificate records on request. An erasure request is logged and routed for legal review; only fields outside the statutory record (such as a contact phone number) can be removed without that review.
