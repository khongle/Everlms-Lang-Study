Reminder rules

The schedule and channels that generate every reminder in the system. Editing a rule here changes what every employee and site training manager sees, everywhere.

Before expiry

T−60 days before expiry Email Recipients: employee
T−30 days before expiry EmailIn-app Recipients: employee
T−7 days before expiry EmailIn-app Push Recipients: employee, site training manager

On and after expiry

On expiry (day 0) EmailIn-app Push Recipients: employee, site training manager
Weekly, while overdue EmailIn-app Recipients: employee, site training manager
Escalate to site training manager Email Recipients: site training manager, HR/HSE Central · fires once, does not repeat

Escalation thresholds

An employee still overdue 30 days after their site training manager was escalated to also notifies HR/HSE Central, so a gap cannot sit unnoticed at one branch indefinitely.

Worked example

SAF-105 First Aid & Emergency Response, Nguyen Van Minh (NEV-004821, NEV-HCM). Certificate expired 28 Jun 2026; today is 15 Jul 2026, 17 days overdue.

  • 29 Apr 2026

    T−60 reminder sent by email to Nguyen Van Minh.

  • 29 May 2026

    T−30 reminder sent by email and in-app.

  • 21 Jun 2026

    T−7 reminder sent by email, in-app, and push, and also to the site training manager (NEV-HCM).

  • 28 Jun 2026

    Certificate expired. On-expiry reminder sent to Nguyen Van Minh and the site training manager (NEV-HCM).

  • 05 Jul & 12 Jul 2026

    Weekly overdue reminders sent.

  • 12 Jul 2026

    Escalated. 14 days overdue: the site training manager (NEV-HCM) was notified directly, in addition to the weekly reminder.

  • 15 Jul 2026 · today

    17 days overdue. Next weekly reminder: 19 Jul 2026. HR/HSE Central escalation is not due until 28 Jul 2026 (30 days overdue).

This is what closes the loopNo one at Nissei manually decides when to remind an employee. These rules generate every reminder and escalation automatically from the training matrix (P036) and each employee’s due dates. The same reminders appear in an employee’s notifications (employee/P019-notifications.html). Turn a rule off here and it stops firing everywhere, immediately, for every branch.
