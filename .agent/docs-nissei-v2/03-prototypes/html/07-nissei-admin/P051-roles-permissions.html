Roles & permissions

Eight roles, six route groups, and the scope each role holds: the dimension that does not exist in a course-selling LMS. Assign a role to a person below; the matrix itself is a read-only reference.

Why scope is a third dimension. The base LMS model has two levels of ownership: your own data, or all data. This product adds branch in between: a role can be handed everything at one factory without being handed every factory. That single addition is why site_training_manager and hr_hse_central exist as two separate roles instead of one “manager” role. See the note below the matrix.

Assign a role

Give an employee one of these roles. This changes who holds a role, not what the role is permitted to do; see the note under the matrix below.

Role assignment is also available from an individual's record at Employees. Platform Administrator is granted outside this system, break-glass only, and cannot be self-service assigned here.

Why two roles, not oneSite Training Manager and HR/HSE Central hold identical Workspace permissions (assign training, manage employees, issue certificates, chase overdue lists) at different scope: one branch versus all five. In the original EverLMS role model both would have been a single “manager” role. Nissei splits them because the branch dimension makes that a meaningfully different grant of access, not a UI preference.
Assign the role, do not define itNissei Administrator can hand this role to a person, using the form above or from an employee's own record. It cannot change what a role is permitted to reach: the rows and columns of the matrix above are product structure, the same as an entity field or a route. Widening a role, for example letting Site Training Manager see a second branch, is a product release, made by the Platform Administrator at Modules and updates, break-glass only. Nothing on this page edits that matrix.

What neither administrator inherits

Day-to-day business work belongs to the business roles either way. Editing the training matrix, approving content or assigning courses is done as HR/HSE Central, not as either administrator. Nissei Administrator is scoped to accounts, access, configuration, editorial content and the audit trail. Platform Administrator is scoped to structure, modules and releases, and is used sparingly, break-glass only. Per the EverLMS least-privilege guidance this product inherits, no single account does business work, content work and platform work all at once.

Where scope is edited

This page is a read view of the role model, plus the form above to assign a role to a person. Branch-level technical scope (timezone, locale, data region) is configured at Branch configuration. An individual's role can also be changed from their own record (see Employees). What a role may reach, the matrix itself, is edited nowhere in this folder.
