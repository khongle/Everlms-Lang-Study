Question 6 · Incorrect

Which document must be available at the point of use for every hazardous chemical on site?

A The purchase invoice
B The Safety Data Sheet (SDS/MSDS)
C The supplier's marketing brochure
D The annual safety training certificate

You selected A. The SDS is the legally required reference at the point of use. It covers handling, storage and emergency measures for that specific chemical.

Question 13 · Incorrect

Two chemicals are marked as incompatible on the storage chart. What is the correct action?

A Store them together, but seal both containers tightly
B Store them in separate, physically segregated areas
C Mix a small quantity of each to test the reaction first
D Store them next to the emergency eyewash station only

You selected A. Sealed containers do not prevent an incompatible reaction if they are damaged or leak. Segregation is the required control.

Question 19 · Incorrect

An employee splashes a chemical in their eyes. What is the first action?

A Call the site nurse and wait for them to arrive
B Rinse the eyes at the emergency eyewash station for at least 15 minutes
C Rub the eyes to remove the chemical
D Apply eye drops from the first aid kit

You selected D. Immediate rinsing at the eyewash station comes first, before any other first aid step. Every minute of delay increases the risk of injury.
