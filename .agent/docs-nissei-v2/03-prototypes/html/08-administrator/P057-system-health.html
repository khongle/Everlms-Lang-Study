What actually sizes this system. Average daily traffic is not the constraint. The whole factory works the same shift pattern, so hundreds of operators can finish a lesson and sit its quiz in the same narrow window, and the nightly compliance recalculation has to walk every one of 5,842 employees’ obligations before the morning shift starts. Quiz-submission concurrency and the nightly recalculation window are the two numbers this platform is sized against, not page views.
