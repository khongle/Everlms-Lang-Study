Why this course is required

Every employee in the job role “Forklift Operator” at Nissei Electric Hanoi is required to hold a current SAF-106 Forklift Operation certificate. This rule comes from the training matrix, not from a manager’s choice: it is one row of the position × course grid maintained centrally by HR/HSE (job role “Forklift Operator” × course “SAF-106”), classified Decree 44/2016 Group 3 and set to a 24-month retraining cycle. It applies to all 64 employees who hold this job role at this branch — the same count shown on the site dashboard and in Assign training.

Prototype noteThe training matrix and master catalog that define this rule belong to HR/HSE Central. A site training manager actions compliance here — assign, book, remind — but does not edit the rule, the course content or its version history.
