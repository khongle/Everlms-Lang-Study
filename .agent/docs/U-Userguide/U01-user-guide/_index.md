# U01-user-guide - User Guide

**For:** learners (students). EverLMS lets you find courses, learn, take quizzes, and earn certificates — on web and the mobile app.

## What you can do
- **Browse & buy:** explore the [catalog](../../C-Design/C11-pages-components/pages/P002-course-catalog.md), filter by category/level, view course details, buy or enroll free, or subscribe to a membership.
- **Learn:** open a course, watch lessons, mark them complete, resume where you left off ([dashboard](../../C-Design/C11-pages-components/pages/P017-dashboard.md)).
- **Get assessed:** take quizzes; see your score and results.
- **Earn certificates:** pass a course's final quiz to auto-receive a downloadable PDF certificate.
- **Engage:** rate courses, post in the forum, take private notes, save courses to your wishlist.
- **Stay updated:** in-app notifications (email/push coming).
