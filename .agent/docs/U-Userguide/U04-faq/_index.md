# U04-faq - FAQ

> Editor-managed FAQ content lives in `lms_faq` (page `/faq`). This is the docs-side reference.

**How do I enroll?** Open a course and click Enroll (free) or buy/subscribe (paid).
**How do I get my certificate?** Complete the course and pass its final quiz — the certificate is issued automatically; download it at `/my-certificates`.
**Can I verify a certificate?** Yes — enter the certificate number at `/certificate/verify`.
**What payment methods are supported?** Card (Stripe) and bank transfer are fully supported; PayPal/ZaloPay may be limited.
**Do I need to buy each course?** Or subscribe to a membership for broad access (`/pricing`).
**Is there a mobile app?** Yes — the EverLMS Flutter app; register with OTP.
**Why didn't I get an email?** Email/push delivery is being completed; in-app notifications always work.
**How do I resume a course?** From "My courses" / dashboard.
