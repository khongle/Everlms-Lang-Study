Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions. Can't find what you need? Contact our support team.

General

What is EverLMS?

EverLMS is a premium online learning platform built on Drupal 11. It connects expert instructors with learners worldwide, offering courses across web development, data science, AI, design, business, and more. All courses include HD video, quizzes, assignments, and a verified completion certificate.

Is EverLMS free to use?

EverLMS offers both free and paid courses. You can create an account for free and immediately access any free-tier courses. Paid courses require either a one-time purchase or a monthly Pro subscription. See our Pricing page for full details.

What languages are courses available in?

The majority of courses are in English. We also have a growing library in Vietnamese, Spanish, and Portuguese. Course language is shown on the course detail page and can be filtered from the browse page.

Is there a mobile app?

A dedicated iOS and Android app is currently in development. In the meantime, EverLMS is fully responsive and works great in any mobile browser, including offline playback via the browser's PWA functionality.

📚

Courses

How long do I have access to a purchased course?

Once you purchase a course, you have lifetime access. You can return to it any time, and all future updates made by the instructor are included at no extra cost.

Can I download videos for offline viewing?

Offline downloads are available on our Pro plan. With a Pro subscription, you can download any course video to your device and watch without an internet connection for up to 30 days before re-sync is required.

What happens if I'm unhappy with a course?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid courses. If you've watched fewer than 30% of the content and request a refund within 30 days of purchase, we'll process it with no questions asked. Submit a refund request from your account dashboard.

Are courses self-paced or scheduled?

All standard courses are fully self-paced — start and finish whenever suits you. Some premium bootcamp cohorts have fixed schedules with live Q&A sessions; these are clearly labelled with a "Cohort" badge on the course listing.

Payment

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, MoMo (Vietnam), VNPay, and bank transfer for enterprise orders. All transactions are processed over TLS and card details are never stored on our servers.

Can I pay in my local currency?

Yes. EverLMS supports 18 currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, VND, INR, AUD, and SGD. Prices are shown in your local currency based on your browser's locale and can be changed from the checkout page.

Is my subscription automatically renewed?

Yes, subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period. You'll receive an email reminder 7 days before renewal. You can cancel at any time from your Account → Billing page and continue access until the end of the current period.

🏅

Certificates

How do I earn a certificate?

Complete all required lessons and pass the final assessment (minimum 70% score). Your certificate is automatically generated and available as a PDF from your dashboard within minutes of completion.

Are EverLMS certificates recognised by employers?

Over 200 partner companies — including tech firms, consultancies, and startups across Southeast Asia and Europe — formally accept EverLMS certificates in their hiring process. A verified certificate link can be added directly to your LinkedIn profile.

How can an employer verify a certificate?

Employers can visit the public certificate verification page and enter the unique code printed on the certificate. No account is required, and the service is free.

Can I retake the final assessment?

Yes. You have unlimited attempts on all assessments. There is a 24-hour cooldown between attempts to encourage proper review of the material.

👤

Account

How do I change my password?

Go to Account → Security → Change Password. You'll need to verify your current password first. If you've forgotten it, use the "Forgot password" link on the login page to receive a reset email.

Can I change my username or email?

Your display name can be changed any time from Account → Profile. Email address changes require re-verification; a confirmation link will be sent to the new address before the change takes effect.

How do I delete my account?

Submit a deletion request from Account → Settings → Delete Account. Your data will be purged within 30 days in compliance with GDPR. Note that certificates you have already issued remain verifiable but are anonymised.

Can I have multiple accounts?

Each person may maintain one learner account. Instructors may hold a separate instructor account in addition to a learner account. Duplicate learner accounts are subject to suspension per our Terms of Service.
