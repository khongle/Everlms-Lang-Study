Frequently Asked Questions

What is a One-Time License? ▼
A one-time license gives you full lifetime access to a single course — including all future updates. You pay once and own the course forever. No subscription required, no recurring charges.
Can I cancel my subscription? ▸
Yes, you can cancel at any time from your account settings. After cancellation, you retain access until the end of your current billing period. No cancellation fees.
Do I keep access after cancellation? ▸
If you subscribed, access ends at the end of your billing period. However, any certificates you earned remain yours permanently and can be verified at any time. For one-time course licenses, access is lifetime regardless of subscription status.
Is there a free trial? ▸
Yes! New users get a 7-day free trial on the Monthly plan. No credit card required to start. You can also access our free course library at any time without any plan.
What payment methods do you accept? ▸
We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfers for annual plans. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption.
