Mission

Learning without limits

We believe technology can dissolve the barriers of geography, language, and budget that have historically gatekept quality education. Our Drupal-powered platform provides instructors with the tools to create world-class content and gives learners a seamless, distraction-free environment to grow their skills.

From beginner tutorials to advanced professional certifications, EverLMS curates every course for depth, accuracy, and real-world applicability.

Vision

A skill-first world by 2030

By 2030 we aim to certify 1 million learners and partner with 50 organisations to recognise EverLMS credentials in hiring pipelines. We are building the trust infrastructure — verified certificates, skill assessments, and employer dashboards — that makes online learning as credible as a university degree.

Every product decision we make is filtered through one question: "Does this make the learner more successful?"

History

Our milestones

2020
Company founded
EverLMS incorporated in Ho Chi Minh City with a seed round and a 3-person team.
2021
First 100 courses live
Launched beta with 50 partner instructors. Reached 5,000 registered learners in the first 6 months.
2023
Certificate verification launched
Introduced blockchain-anchored certificates and a public verification portal used by 200+ employers.
2025
Series A & global expansion
Closed $4.5M Series A. Expanded to 58 countries and migrated platform to Drupal 11.
2026
Learning Paths 2.0 — today
Structured multi-course paths with adaptive quizzes, mentor sessions, and employer-verified skill badges.
