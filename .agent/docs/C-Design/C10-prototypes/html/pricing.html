Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about EverLMS plans and billing.

Can I try Pro before paying?

Yes. Every new account gets a 7-day free Pro trial with full access to all 1,200+ courses, certificates, and mentoring — no credit card required. You only need to add payment details if you decide to continue after the trial.

What happens when my trial ends?

At the end of your 7-day trial you will be asked to choose a plan. If you do not upgrade, your account automatically reverts to the Free plan. You keep any progress you have made and retain access to free courses — you simply lose Pro-only features like certificates and offline downloads.

Can I switch plans or cancel any time?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. There are no lock-in contracts or cancellation fees. If you cancel a paid plan mid-cycle, you keep Pro access until the end of the billing period.

Does the annual plan really save 20%?

Yes. Choosing annual billing locks in a 20% discount compared to paying month-to-month. For Pro that works out to $278.40 per year instead of $348 — a saving of $69.60. Annual plans are billed as a single upfront payment.

How does the Teams plan work for larger organisations?

The Teams plan includes up to 20 seats at $99 per month. For larger organisations — or if you need custom integrations, dedicated customer success, or volume pricing — please contact our sales team via the Contact page and we will tailor a plan for you.

Are certificates recognised by employers?

EverLMS certificates include a unique verification link that employers can check instantly. Our courses are designed around real-world job requirements and our instructors are industry practitioners. Many of our learners have used EverLMS certificates to land roles at global companies — see learner stories on the About page.
