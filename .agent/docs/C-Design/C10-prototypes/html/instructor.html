About Sarah

Turning real-world engineering into clear, actionable lessons

Sarah Chen spent 12 years building web products at Google and a string of high-growth startups before trading her IDE for a screen recorder. She shipped features used by hundreds of millions of people, contributed to open-source tooling adopted across the industry, and mentored dozens of engineers from junior to senior.

That depth of real-world experience flows directly into her teaching. Sarah specialises in React, Next.js, TypeScript, and the broader modern JavaScript ecosystem — covering not just the "how" but the "why" behind every pattern and trade-off. Her students consistently describe her explanations as the clearest they have encountered anywhere.

When she is not recording, Sarah writes about software architecture on her blog, speaks at conferences, and reviews open-source pull requests. She is passionate about making professional-grade engineering education accessible to everyone, regardless of background.

  • React & Next.js — App Router, server components, RSC patterns, and performance optimisation.
  • Modern JavaScript & TypeScript — ES2024 features, type safety, and scalable architecture.
  • Full-stack development — API design, databases, authentication, and cloud deployment.
  • AI-assisted engineering — integrating LLMs, prompt engineering, and AI tooling into real products.
  • Engineering best practices — code review, testing, CI/CD, and professional developer workflows.
