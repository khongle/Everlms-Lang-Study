Common questions

Frequently asked

Can't find the answer you need? Send us a message above or email hello@everlms.com.

How quickly will I receive a reply?

Our support team aims to respond to all enquiries within one business day during our listed support hours. For billing and account access issues, we prioritise within four hours. You'll receive a confirmation email as soon as your message is received.

I'm having trouble accessing my enrolled courses — what should I do?

First, try logging out and back in at the login page. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache or try a different browser. If you still can't access a course you've purchased, contact us with your order confirmation email and we'll resolve it immediately.

How do I apply to become an EverLMS instructor?

Visit our Become an instructor page and complete the application form. We review all applications within five business days. You'll need to share a short video introduction and an outline of your proposed course. Approved instructors earn a 70% revenue share and get access to our AI Course Builder.

Can I request a refund for a course I've purchased?

Yes. EverLMS offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid course purchases, no questions asked. Simply send us your order number and the email address used at checkout, and we'll process the refund within three to five business days. Refunds are not available for Team plan seats after more than 50% of the billing period has elapsed.
