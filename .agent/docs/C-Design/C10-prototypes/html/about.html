Our mission

We believe skills should be within
everyone's reach

EverLMS was built on a simple conviction: the right knowledge, taught by the right people, can change a career — and a life. We're making world-class education accessible, affordable and effective for learners in every corner of the globe.

What drives us

Learning that opens doors — for everyone

Too many talented people are locked out of opportunity simply because quality education costs too much or lives too far away. We started EverLMS to tear down that barrier.

We partner with the world's best practitioners — engineers, designers, analysts, entrepreneurs — and give them the tools to share their expertise at scale. Every course on EverLMS is built to deliver real, job-ready skills, not just certificates.

From a software developer in Nairobi to a student in Ho Chi Minh City, we want every learner to have access to the same quality of instruction as someone sitting in a Silicon Valley classroom.

How we got here

Our story

From a rented garage to a global platform — this is the story of EverLMS.

  • 2019

    Founded in a garage

    Three engineers frustrated by the cost of quality online education pool their savings and launch EverLMS from a rented garage in Austin, Texas. The first 12 courses go live with a handful of passionate beta learners.

  • 2021

    Reached 100,000 learners

    Word spreads. EverLMS crosses 100,000 enrolled learners across 60 countries. We raise our Series A, triple the team, and open the platform to independent instructors for the first time.

  • 2023

    Launched the instructor marketplace

    The marketplace goes live, letting any expert apply to create and sell courses. Within six months, 200+ instructors join, and learner numbers surpass 250,000. Average instructor earnings exceed $18,000 per year.

  • 2026

    Shipped the AI Course Builder

    EverLMS launches its AI Course Builder, allowing instructors to generate a full course outline, learning objectives and quiz questions in minutes. We hit 480,000 active learners and expand our content library to 1,200+ courses across 8 categories.

What we stand for

Our values

Every product decision, every hire, every partnership runs through the same filter: does it help more people learn?

Access without limits

Quality education should not be a privilege. We offer free courses, sliding-scale pricing and mobile-first design so no learner is left behind — regardless of location, bandwidth or budget.

Learn by doing

We believe knowledge only sticks when you apply it. Every course on EverLMS includes hands-on projects, coding playgrounds or creative exercises — because watching is not enough.

Outcomes, not just certificates

We measure success by what learners do after they graduate — promotions earned, projects shipped, businesses launched. Certificates follow outcomes; they don't replace them.

Trust & transparency

Honest course ratings, clear pricing, no hidden fees. We show you exactly what you are paying for, and we stand behind every course with a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Instructor partnership

We share 70% of revenue with our instructors because their expertise is the product. When our instructors thrive, learners get better content — a flywheel that raises the bar for everyone.

Global mindset

With learners in 190 countries, we build for the world. Every interface decision, every payment option and every support channel is chosen with our most underserved markets in mind.
