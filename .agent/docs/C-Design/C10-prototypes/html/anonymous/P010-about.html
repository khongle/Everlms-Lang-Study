Our story

From a side project to a global classroom

  • 2016

    Founded in San Francisco

    Three engineers, frustrated with clunky corporate training tools, started building EverLMS as a weekend project to teach coworkers to code.

  • 2018

    50,000 learners, first mobile app

    We shipped offline downloads and a native mobile app so learners could keep their streak going on the commute.

  • 2020

    Certificates and mentoring launch

    Verified certificates and monthly instructor mentoring arrived alongside Pro membership, and the catalog crossed 500 courses.

  • 2022

    EverLMS for Teams

    Organizations could now buy seats in bulk with team analytics, SSO and priority support for their learning programs.

  • 2026

    480,000+ learners, 190 countries

    Today EverLMS hosts 1,200+ courses from 350+ instructors, with a 92% completion rate well above the industry average.

What we believe

The values behind every course

Learner-first

Every roadmap decision starts with completion rate and learner outcomes, not vanity metrics like enrollment count.

Quality over quantity

Every instructor is vetted and every course is reviewed before publishing — we would rather have 1,200 great courses than 10,000 mediocre ones.

Access for everyone

A free tier, low-bandwidth mobile support and scholarships keep the door open regardless of budget or connectivity.
