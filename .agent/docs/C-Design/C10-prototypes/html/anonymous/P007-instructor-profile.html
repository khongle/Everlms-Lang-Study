About Sarah

Sarah spent over a decade as a senior software engineer at Google, shipping front-end infrastructure used by millions of people, before moving into full-time teaching in 2019. She holds an MS in Computer Science and has mentored more than 200 engineers through internal Google programs.

Her flagship course, Complete Modern React & Next.js Bootcamp, has become one of EverLMS's best-sellers, praised for its emphasis on production-grade patterns — testing, performance, deployment — rather than toy examples. Sarah answers questions in the course community most weekdays and hosts a monthly live Q&A for Pro members.
