Pricing FAQ

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Pro and Teams are billed monthly or annually with no long-term contract — cancel from your membership settings and keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Is there a free trial for Pro?

The Free plan lets you try one full learning track with no time limit and no credit card. When you are ready for certificates and the full catalog, upgrade to Pro at any time.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards, plus PayPal. Teams plans can also be invoiced annually for larger organizations.

Can I switch between Pro and Teams later?

Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time from your membership page. Changes are pro-rated automatically against your current billing cycle.

Do you offer a student or non-profit discount?

Yes — verified students and registered non-profits get 30% off Pro. Contact our support team after signing up to apply the discount to your account.

What is your refund policy?

If Pro or Teams is not right for you, contact support within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund, no questions asked.
